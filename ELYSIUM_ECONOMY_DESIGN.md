# Elysium — Currency & Economy Design (Master Document)

**Status: LIVE DOCUMENT — same discipline as the class and floor design

**Cross-reference:** ties directly into ELYSIUM_CLASS_DESIGN.md's "Lore —

---

## Design Philosophy

**Three currencies is the sweet spot.** More starts feeling like a mobile
game. Fewer makes every activity feel the same.

**Design rule — locked:** No currency should ever become obsolete. Even
at Floor 10, players should still have reasons to earn all three. This
avoids the common MMO problem of old currencies becoming worthless.

---

## The Three Currencies (LOCKED — final names)

### ⚒️ Valkor's Sigils
**Purpose:** Equipment upgrades (blacksmith).

**Examples:** +1, +2, +3, weapon evolution, armor evolution.

**Obtained from:** Dungeon bosses, elite monsters, repeatable dungeons,
labyrinths (ties directly into ELYSIUM_FLOOR_DESIGN.md's per-floor
Repeatable Dungeon / Labyrinth content).

**Framing:** the player's main progression currency. God association:
Valkor = Strength/War/Power.

---

### ✨ Stacia's Essence
**Purpose:** Enchanting and rerolling equipment.

**Examples:** +Critical Rate, +Attack, +Strength, +Movement Speed.

**Obtained from:** Disenchanting equipment, rare monsters, world events,
chests.

**Framing:** makes unwanted gear valuable — instead of selling it, you
salvage it into this currency. God association: Stacia = Magic/Grace.

---

### 👑 Elysian Tokens
**Purpose:** Cosmetics ONLY. Never tied to raw power progression.

**Examples:** Mounts, costumes, hairstyles, weapon skins, titles, emotes,
housing decorations.

**Obtained from:** Seasonal events, achievements, floor clears, weekly
objectives.

**Framing:** Elysium = Prestige.

## Naming Evolution (for context/history — not currently in use)

Earlier candidate names considered before landing on the god-tied final
set, kept here for reference only:
- Forge Marks → considered: Ember Shards, Titan Shards, Forgestones, Anvil
  Fragments → **superseded by Valkor's Sigils**
- Arcane Dust → considered: Astral Dust, Mana Crystal, Ether Dust, Aether
  Dust, Runic Powder, Soul Dust → **superseded by Stacia's Essence**
- Cosmetic currency → considered: Astral Coin, Stacia's Blessing, Hero
  Medals, Legacy Coins → **superseded by Elysian Tokens**

## Open Questions / Not Yet Decided

1. Exact drop rates/sources per floor — "dungeon bosses, elite monsters,
   repeatable dungeons, labyrinths" is the source list for Valkor's
   Sigils, but exact rates/amounts per floor aren't specified yet.
2. Disenchant ratio for Stacia's Essence — how much Essence a given piece
   of gear yields when salvaged isn't defined yet.
3. Cross-reference to ELYSIUM_FLOOR_DESIGN.md's open item about the
   Labyrinth's "rare materials"/"treasure vaults" — do these specifically
   drop Valkor's Sigils, Stacia's Essence, both, or separate
   crafting-material items distinct from these three currencies?
4. "No currency ever obsolete" design rule — worth defining CONCRETELY
   what Floor 10 players spend Valkor's Sigils/Stacia's Essence on, since
   "keep earning them" only avoids obsolescence if there's also a
   meaningful sink at end-game (e.g. does gear keep having higher upgrade
   tiers indefinitely, or does something else consume these at Floor 10?).
